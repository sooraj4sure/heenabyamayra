# Heena by Amayra — Demo v1

Demo website for **Heena by Amayra**, a bridal & festival mehndi artist in
Diyodar, Gujarat (Amayra Shaikh · @aamyrashaikh098 · +91 63561 45798).

Built for Evenlyn Studio. See `RESEARCH.md` for the research brief, sources
and everything still unverified (prices, service area beyond Diyodar, timings).

## Pages

- `/` — Home: hero, services preview, gallery preview, booking steps, about
  teaser, CTA
- `/designs` — Filterable gallery (Bridal / Festival / Arabic & modern) with
  a lightbox and per-style WhatsApp enquiry
- `/services` — Bridal, Festival, Home Service, Family & Group + styles menu
  + what to share when booking
- `/about` — About Amayra (warm first-person demo copy, facts only)
- `/contact` — WhatsApp / Instagram DM / Email cards + booking steps

## Rules followed

- No prices anywhere — "Price on enquiry" / "Enquire on WhatsApp".
