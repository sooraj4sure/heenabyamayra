## The niche — Gujarat bridal / festival mehndi

- **Gujarati styles brides ask for:** Kutch embroidery-inspired patterns,
  dandiya & elephant motifs, Arabic designs (fine detailing, bold patterns),
  plus Rajasthani and Indo-Arabic fusion and simple contemporary styles
  (dreamweddinghub.com, Gujarat mehendi-artist guide).
- **What brides check before booking:** portfolio photos, reviews from real
  customers, whether the artist offers home service; book 1–2 months ahead
  in wedding season (same guide).
- **Home service is the norm for this niche in Gujarat:** competitor artists
  (Surat, Ahmedabad) all lead with doorstep service, natural henna, advance
  booking, custom designs (initials, motifs), on-time arrival.
- **Gujarat price context (NOT used on the demo — no prices are published
  for Amayra):** guides quote roughly ₹2,000–₹12,000 for mehndi artists in
  Gujarat depending on design and occasion; bridal full-hand costs more than
  simple party designs. Competitor sites elsewhere publish bridal packages
  from ~₹5,000 upward. All demo CTAs are "Enquire on WhatsApp" — Amayra
  sets her own prices.
- **Festivals where mehndi is booked:** weddings, engagements, sangeet,
  Karva Chauth, Teej, Eid, Diwali, Rakhi, baby showers, family functions.
